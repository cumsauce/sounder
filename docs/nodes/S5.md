# S5: transient, gate, sub, exciter

Measured numbers are in the table at the end, from
`crates/sfx_analysis/tests/dynamics.rs`
(`cargo test -p sfx_analysis --test dynamics -- --nocapture`).

## `transient`

A dynamics node: separate gain for the attack and the sustain of a sound.

### Fields

| Field | Type | Default | Range / unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `input` | signal | required | mono or stereo audio | Stereo channels are linked (one gain). |
| `attack_db` | number | 0 | -24 .. 24 dB | Gain while the fast follower is above the slow one. |
| `sustain_db` | number | 0 | -24 .. 24 dB | Gain while the level is falling (fast follower below the slow one). A steady plateau is not touched. |
| `speed` | number | 0.02 | 0.001 .. 1 s | Fast follower time constant `speed / 4`, slow `speed * 2.5`. |

Both followers track the power of the input. Their difference in dB, divided
by 6 and clamped to 0..1 (positive: rising) or 0..1 (negative: falling), weights `attack_db` and
`sustain_db`; at equal levels the gain is exactly 1. Values past the ranges
error with the limit in the message.

### Recipe

```json
[
  {"type": "transient", "name": "t", "input": "src", "attack_db": 6, "sustain_db": -3, "speed": 0.02}
]
```

Give the human macros on `attack_db` (claim `crest`, direction 1) and
`sustain_db`.

### Measured

See the table at the end.

### Honest limits

- Low tones: the power followers ripple at twice the tone frequency, so a
  steady sine below roughly 100 Hz is gain-modulated slightly. Filter the
  low end out before it, or raise `speed`.
- A steady signal is untouched (gain 1); only changes in level are shaped.
- Out-of-range extremes (attack/sustain +-24 dB, speed 0.001 and 1) stay
  finite (test `transient_finite_at_extremes_and_errors`).
- Not heard.

## `gate`

A dynamics node: downward expander / noise gate with hold and a sidechain key.

### Fields

| Field | Type | Default | Range / unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `input` | signal | required | mono or stereo audio | Stereo channels linked. |
| `threshold_db` | number | required | dBFS, peak | Under it the gate closes. |
| `ratio` | number | left out = full gate | >= 1 | Each dB under the threshold becomes `ratio` dB; 120 dB reduction at most. 1 changes nothing. |
| `attack` | number | 0.001 | 0 .. 10 s | Time constant of the gain opening. |
| `release` | number | 0.05 | 0 .. 10 s | Time constant of the gain closing (60 dB takes about 6.9 times it). |
| `hold` | number | 0.01 | 0 .. 10 s | Stays open this long after the level last reached the threshold. |
| `key` | signal | the input | mono or stereo audio | Drives the detector; the gain applies to `input`. |

The detector is a peak follower (instant attack, 5 ms release). The gain
starts at its first target, so a gate that begins closed does not fade in.

### Recipe

```json
[
  {"type": "gate", "name": "g", "input": "src", "threshold_db": -40, "attack": 0.001, "release": 0.05, "hold": 0.01}
]
```

Give the human a macro on `release` (claim `decay`, direction 1).

### Measured

See the table at the end.

### Honest limits

- The detector follows peaks, so a signal at the threshold with a period
  longer than the hold (very low tones) can chatter; raise `hold`.
- A finite `ratio` expands by the detector level (peak with a 5 ms release),
  which sits about 0.4 dB under the true peak of a steady tone.
- No lookahead: the first milliseconds of a burst are cut by `attack`.
- Extremes (threshold -200 or 0, ratio 1000, attack/release/hold 0 or 10 s)
  stay finite (test `gate_deterministic_finite_and_errors`).
- Not heard.

## `sub`

A synthesis node: adds a sine one or two octaves under the low band of the input.

### Fields

| Field | Type | Default | Range / unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `input` | signal | required | mono audio | A stereo input errors: add a `mix` with `"to": "mono"`. |
| `octaves` | number | 1 | 1 or 2 | Frequency divided by 2 or 4. Other values error. |
| `mix` | number | 0.5 | 0 .. 4 | Sub level added to the input; 0 returns the input bit for bit. |
| `lowpass` | number | 120 | 20 .. 1000 Hz | 4th-order low-pass whose zero crossings are divided. |

Chain: low-pass (Butterworth 4th order), Schmitt zero-crossing detector (10 %
of the band level), one or two flip-flops, two 2nd-order low-passes at 1.5 x
the sub's measured frequency (square to sine), scaled by the amplitude of the
input low-passed at 4 x `lowpass`. The output is `input + mix * sub`.

Decision: the amplitude follows the input's band under 4 x `lowpass`, not the
full-band envelope, so a bright sound with no bass does not get a loud sub.

### Recipe

```json
[
  {"type": "sub", "name": "s", "input": "tone", "octaves": 1, "mix": 0.5, "lowpass": 250}
]
```

Give the human a macro on `mix` (claim `lufs`, direction 1).

### Measured

See the table at the end.

### Honest limits

- Only content under about `lowpass` (plus what survives the filter) gets a
  sub; a note well above it is divided unreliably. Raise `lowpass` to the
  note's frequency.
- Monophonic: chords or noise give an irregular divider.
- The sub follows the input's pitch with a delay of the filters (a few ms).
- Extremes (lowpass 20 and 1000, two octaves, mix 4, noise, DC, 9 kHz square)
  stay finite (test `sub_deterministic_finite_and_errors`).
- Not heard.

## `exciter`

A distortion node: saturates the band above `freq` and adds it back.

### Fields

| Field | Type | Default | Range / unit | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `input` | signal | required | mono or stereo audio | Each channel on its own. |
| `freq` | number | 3000 | 20 .. 0.45 x sample rate Hz | Corner of both 2nd-order high-pass filters. |
| `drive` | number | 2 | > 0 .. 100 | Gain into `tanh`. |
| `mix` | number | 0.3 | 0 .. 10 | Level of the saturated band added; 0 returns the input bit for bit. |

Output: `input + mix * hp(tanh(drive * hp(input)))`. The saturator is odd, so
the new content is odd harmonics.

### Recipe

```json
[
  {"type": "exciter", "name": "x", "input": "src", "freq": 3000, "drive": 2, "mix": 0.3}
]
```

Give the human a macro on `mix` (claim `centroid`, direction 1).

### Measured

See the table at the end.

### Honest limits

- No oversampling: harmonics above Nyquist fold back (aliasing measured in
  `exciter_mix_zero_is_the_input_and_aliasing_is_reported`). Put the exciter
  below a `shape` with `oversample` if that matters, or keep `drive` low.
- The first high-pass is 2nd order, so content just under `freq` is attenuated
  but not removed before saturation.
- Extremes (freq 20 and 21600, drive 0.0001 and 100, mix 10, white noise)
  stay finite (test `exciter_deterministic_finite_stereo_and_errors`).
- Not heard.
