# Batch 6: the Farnell ports (2026-11-19)

25 designs from the 14 new nodes, each dry and in a small room, rated blind
(`tools/labels/gen6.py`, key `batch6.json`). Mostly node defaults.

**Heard as what they are (good):** `helicopter` (also at `distance` 0.7:
"distant helicopter"), `fly`, `transporter` ("teleporter"), `phone_tone`
dial / busy / ringback / dtmf, `two_tone` ("phone", "phone error"),
`phaser_beam` ("alert or futuristic sound", "shrill alarm"), `rain`
`surface: water` ("bubbles").

**Good, but heard as something else:** `cicada` buzz as "rattlesnake",
call as "scissors snipping"; `stream` at `flow` 2, `layers` 4 as "fluid
gurgling in a small tube" / "stomach rumbling"; `rain` leaf in the room as
"crunching gravel".

**Ok:** `droid` ("robot beeps", "too random"), `stream` default ("aquaphone
bubbles"), `pour` ("weird bubbles in a chemistry set"), `torpedo` ("space
weapon"), `phone_tone` pulse_dial ("distant automatic gunfire").

**Bad / not rain:** `rain` leaf dry ("static"), ground ("tape stretching",
"ripping fabric"), window ("popcorn"). None of the four surfaces reads as
rain: rain stays a gap.

**`roar` reads as a cow** every time: "the pitch envelope is too digital -
goes up then immediately down perfectly with no wavering". Next step: jitter
and slow wander on the pitch envelope, more noise in the source. `rocket`
reads as "a video game weapon", fake.
